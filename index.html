Hi, I'm Andre, an M.S. student in Scientific Computing at NYU Courant advised by Lerrel Pinto. I'm part of NYU's five-year BA/MS program and earned my B.A. in Mathematics and Computer Science in 2026, graduating Phi Beta Kappa.

My research focuses on tactile sensing, hardware–software co-design, and dexterous manipulation.

I’m an optimistic roboticist in the age of coding agents. I started learning the violin at seven, so my test of physical intelligence is a personal one: hand a robot a sheet of music and a violin, and let it take it from there. Until then, I have work to do.

I'm seeking robotics roles and applying to PhD programs in the 2026 application cycle.
